$ purpose

We are here to de-mystify and democratize frontier model development. The methods behind large models are not magic — they are recipes, and recipes can be published, replicated, and taught. garagelm exists to prove the whole loop runs on hardware anyone can buy: train it, measure it honestly, release it, and show every number along the way — while pushing how much intelligence a watt of electricity and an hour of training can buy.
